# Civilian Crews on Auxiliary Vessels Direction

Direction Made Pursuant to Section 224 of the National Defence Act Respecting Civilian Crews on Auxiliary Vessels

## Short Title

### Section 1

This Direction may be cited as the Civilian Crews on Auxiliary Vessels Direction.

## Application

### Section 2

It is hereby directed that the Government Vessels Discipline Act shall apply to civilian crews engaged for service on auxiliary vessels of the Canadian Forces.
