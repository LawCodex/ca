# Specification of Statutory Provisions (Treasury Board) Order

Order Specifying the Provisions of Acts Respecting a Matter in Relation to which the Treasury Board is Authorized to Exercise the Powers of the Governor in Council

## Short Title

### Section 1

This Order may be cited as the Specification of Statutory Provisions (Treasury Board) Order.

## Specification

### Section 2

The Treasury Board is authorized to exercise the powers of the Governor in Council under the provisions specified in the schedule.

## SCHEDULE (Section 2)

### Section 1

Subsection 8(1) and subsection 9(1) of the Canadian Wheat Board Act.

### Section 2

Subsection 11(3) of the Central Mortgage and Housing Corporation Act in relation to the establishment and operation of a pension fund under subsection 14(3) of that Act.

### Section 3

Subsection 6(5) and section 7 of the Diplomatic Service (Special) Superannuation Act and section 16 of that Act in relation to the powers of the Governor in Council under subsection 6(5) and section 7 of that Act.

### Section 4

Subsection 5(1) of the Government Companies Operation Act.

### Section 5

Section 44 of the Public Service Employment Act.

### Section 6

Finance Vote 462 of the Appropriation Act, No. 5, 1943.

### Section 7

Finance Vote 124 of the Appropriation Act, No. 6, 1960.

### Section 8

Finance Vote 686 of the Appropriation Act, No. 2, 1961.

### Section 9

Finance vote 632 of the Appropriation Act, No. 2, 1962.

### Section 10

Finance Vote 59a of the Appropriation Act, No. 5, 1963.

### Section 11

Finance Vote 63a of the Appropriation Act, No. 5, 1963.

### Section 12

Finance Vote 71a of the Appropriation Act, No. 5, 1963.

### Section 13

Finance Vote 20b of the Appropriation Act, No. 10, 1964.

### Section 14

Finance Vote 20a of the Appropriation Act, No. 9, 1966.
