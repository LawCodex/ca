# L-5 hydroxytryptophan Remission Order

Order Respecting the Remission of Customs Duties on L-5 Hydroxytryptophan

## Short Title

### Section 1

This Order may be cited as the L-5 hydroxytryptophan Remission Order.

## Remission

### Section 2

Remission is hereby granted of the customs duties paid or payable under the Customs Tariff on L-5 hydroxytryptophan imported on or after October 1, 1975.

*SI/88-17, s. 2(E)*
