# National Energy Board Order No. MO-62-69

Order Respecting the Application of Part IV of the National Energy Board Act

## Short Title

### Section 1

This Order may be cited as the National Energy Board Order No. MO-62-69.

## Application of part IV of the act

### Section 2

The National Energy Board, pursuant to subsection 97(1)<sup>1</sup> of the National Energy Board Act, hereby orders that Part IV of the said Act applies to every person who was operating a pipeline on November 2, 1959.

<sup>1</sup> This subsection is not reproduced in the R.S.C., 1970, see S.C., 1959, c. 46.
