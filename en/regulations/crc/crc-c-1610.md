# Game Declared in Danger of Becoming Extinct

## Declaration

### Section 1

The game listed in the schedule is hereby declared to be game in danger of becoming extinct pursuant to subsection 17(3) of the Yukon Act.

## SCHEDULE

- Bison (buffalo)

- Black-tailed deer

- Elk

- Musk-oxen
