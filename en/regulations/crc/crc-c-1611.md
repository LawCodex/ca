# Oaths of Allegiance and Office Order (Yukon)

## Section 1

[Repealed, SOR/2003-116, s. 23]

## Oath of Allegiance

### Section 2

The oath of allegiance prescribed by the Oaths of Allegiance Act is the oath of allegiance to be taken and subscribed by the Commissioner of Yukon.

*SOR/2003-116, s. 24*

## Oath of Office

### Section 3

The oath of office to be taken and subscribed by the Commissioner of Yukon is set out in Schedule I.

*SOR/2003-116, s. 28*

### Section 4

[Repealed, SOR/2003-116, s. 25]

## Swearing

### Section 5

The oath of allegiance and oath of office of the Commissioner of Yukon shall be sworn and subscribed before a judge of the Supreme Court of the Yukon Territory or, in his absence, a police magistrate.

*SOR/2003-116, s. 28*

### Section 6

[Repealed, SOR/2003-116, s. 26]

## SCHEDULE I — Oath of Office for Commissioner of Yukon (Section 3)

I, … , do solemnly and sincerely promise and swear that I will duly and faithfully and to the best of my skill and knowledge execute the powers and trust reposed in me as the Commissioner of Yukon. So help me God.

… …
