# Order Designating the Commission on the Future of Health Care in Canada as a Department and the Prime Minister as Appropriate Minister

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, hereby

- **(a)** pursuant to paragraph (b)<sup>a</sup> of the definition ***department*** in section 2 of the Financial Administration Act, designates the Commission on the Future of Health Care in Canada as a department for the purposes of that Act; and

- **(b)** pursuant to paragraph (b)<sup>b</sup> of the definition ***appropriate Minister*** in section 2 of that Act, designates the Prime Minister as the appropriate Minister with respect to the Commission on the Future of Health Care in Canada.

  <sup>a</sup> S.C. 1992, c. 1, s. 69(3)

  <sup>b</sup> S.C. 1992, c. 1, s. 69(1)
