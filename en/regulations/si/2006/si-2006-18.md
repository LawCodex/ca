# Order Designating the Office of the Registrar of Lobbyists as a Department and the Registrar as Deputy Head

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, hereby

- **(a)** pursuant to paragraph (b) of the definition *department* in subsection 2(1) of the Public Service Employment Act<sup>a</sup>, designates the Office of the Registrar of Lobbyists as a department for the purposes of that Act, and

- **(b)** pursuant to paragraph (b) of the definition *deputy head* in subsection 2(1) of the Public Service Employment Act<sup>a</sup>, designates the Registrar of the Office of the Registrar of Lobbyists as the deputy head of that office for the purposes of that Act,

effective February 6, 2006.

<sup>a</sup> S.C. 2003, c. 22, ss. 12, 13
