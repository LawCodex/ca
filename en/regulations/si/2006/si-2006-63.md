# Order Designating the Executive Director of the Public Appointments Commission Secretariat as Deputy Head

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 11(2)(a)<sup>a</sup> of the Financial Administration Act, hereby designates the Executive Director of the Public Appointments Commission Secretariat as deputy head in respect of that Secretariat.

<sup>a</sup> S.C. 2003, c. 22, s. 8
