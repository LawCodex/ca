# Order Designating the President of the King’s Privy Council for Canada as the Minister for the Purposes of the Building Canada Act

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, under section 3 of the Building Canada Act<sup>a</sup>, designates the President of the King’s Privy Council for Canada, a member of the King’s Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of the Building Canada Act.

<sup>a</sup> S.C. 2025, c. 2, s. 4
