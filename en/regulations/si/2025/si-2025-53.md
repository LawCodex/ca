# Order Designating the Leader of the Government in the House of Commons for the Purposes of the Canada Elections Act

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, under the definition *Minister* in subsection 2(1) of the Canada Elections Act<sup>a</sup>,

<sup>a</sup> S.C. 2000, c. 9

- **(a)** repeals Order in Council P.C. 2025-86 of February 12, 2025<sup>b</sup>; and

  <sup>b</sup> SI/2025-16

- **(b)** designates the Leader of the Government in the House of Commons, a member of the King’s Privy Council for Canada, for the purposes of that Act.
