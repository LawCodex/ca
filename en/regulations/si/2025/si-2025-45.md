# Order Designating the Minister of Canadian Heritage as the Minister for the Purposes of the Canadian Multiculturalism Act

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, under the definition *Minister* in section 2 of the Canadian Multiculturalism Act<sup>a</sup>,

<sup>a</sup> R.S., c. 24 (4th Supp.)

- **(a)** repeals Order in Council P.C. 2023-771 of July 26, 2023<sup>b</sup>; and

  <sup>b</sup> SI/2023-41

- **(b)** designates the Minister of Canadian Heritage, a member of the King’s Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of that Act.
