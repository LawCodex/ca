# Order Transferring from the Minister of State (Official Languages) to the Minister of Canadian Heritage the Powers, Duties and Functions Under the Official Languages Act

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, under paragraph 2(a)<sup>a</sup> of the Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act<sup>b</sup>, transfers to the Minister of Canadian Heritage the powers, duties and functions under the Official Languages Act<sup>c</sup> that were transferred to the Minister of State (Official Languages) by Order in Council P.C. 2021-946 of October 26, 2021<sup>d</sup>.

<sup>a</sup> S.C. 2003, c. 22, s. 207

<sup>b</sup> R.S., c. P-34

<sup>c</sup> R.S.C. 1985, c. 31 (4th Supp.)

<sup>d</sup> SI/2021-91
