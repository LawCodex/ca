# Order Designating the Department of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness to Provide Support to the Minister of Emergency Management and Community Resilience

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, under subsection 5(1)<sup>a</sup> of the Salaries Act<sup>b</sup>, designates the Department of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness to provide support to the Minister of Emergency Management and Community Resilience in the carrying out of that Minister’s responsibilities.

<sup>a</sup> S.C. 2018, c. 18, s. 3

<sup>b</sup> R.S., c. S-3
