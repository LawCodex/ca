# Order Designating the Minister of Communications as Appropriate Minister with Respect to the National Battlefields Commission

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph (c.1)<sup>\*</sup> of the definition ***appropriate Minister*** in section 2 of the Financial Administration Act, is pleased hereby

<sup>*</sup> S.C. 1992, c. 1, s. 69(2)

- **(a)** to revoke Order in Council P.C. 1993-1452 of June 25, 1993<sup>\*\*</sup>; and

  <sup>**</sup> SI/93-106, 1993 Canada Gazette Part II, p. 3198

- **(b)** to designate the Minister of Communications, a member of the Queen’s Privy Council for Canada, as the appropriate Minister with respect to the National Battlefields Commission for the purposes of the Financial Administration Act.
