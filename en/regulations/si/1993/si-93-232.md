# Order Designating the Minister of Communications as Minister for Purposes of Section 47 of the Act

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to section 47<sup>\*</sup> of the Public Service Employment Act, is pleased hereby to designate the Minister of Communications, a member of the Queen’s Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of section 47 of the Public Service Employment Act.

<sup>*</sup> S.C. 1992, c. 54, s. 28
