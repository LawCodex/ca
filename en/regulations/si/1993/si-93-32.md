# Minister of Communications Authority to Prescribe Fees Order

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Communications and the Treasury Board, pursuant to paragraph 19(1)(b)<sup>\*</sup> of the Financial Administration Act, is pleased hereby to authorize the Minister of Communications to prescribe, by order, the fees or charges to be paid by a user for whom the Department of Communications provides any of the following services :

<sup>*</sup> S.C. 1991, c. 24, s. 6

- **(a)** the technical assessment, reassessment, testing or certification of telecommunications apparatus; and

- **(b)** the labelling or marking of telecommunications apparatus as evidence of ongoing compliance with the technical requirements, specifications, standards or procedures of the Department of Communications.
