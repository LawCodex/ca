# Order Designating the Minister of Industry, Science and Technology as Minister for Purposes of the Act

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to the definition ***Minister*** in section 2 of the Canadian Space Agency Act<sup>\*</sup>, is pleased hereby

<sup>*</sup> S.C. 1990, c. 13

- **(a)** to revoke Order in Council P.C. 1993-1672 of August 18, 1993<sup>\*\*</sup>;

  <sup>**</sup> SI/93-172, 1993 Canada Gazette Part II, p. 3711

- **(b)** to designate the Minister of Industry, Science and Technology, a member of the Queen’s Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of that Act.
