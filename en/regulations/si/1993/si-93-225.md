# Order Designating the Commission of Inquiry on the Blood System in Canada as a Department and the Prime Minister as Appropriate Minister

His Excellency the Governor General In Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, is pleased hereby

- **(a)** pursuant to paragraph (b) of the definition ***department*** in section 2 of the Financial Administration Act, to designate the Commission of Inquiry on the Blood System in Canada as a department for the purposes of that Act; and

- **(b)** pursuant to paragraph (b) of the definition ***appropriate Minister*** in section 2 of the Financial Administration Act, to designate the Prime Minister as the appropriate Minister with respect to the Commission of Inquiry on the Blood System in Canada.
