# Order Designating the Minister of Industry, Science and Technology as Minister for Purposes of the Standards Council of Canada Act

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to the definition ***Minister*** in section 2 of the Standards Council of Canada Act, is pleased hereby

- **(a)** to revoke Order in Council P.C. 1982-15 of January 12, 1982<sup>\*</sup>; and

  <sup>*</sup> SI/82-28, 1982 Canada Gazette Part II, p. 424

  - **(b)** to designate the Minister of Industry, Science and Technology, a member of the Queen’s Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of the Standards Council of Canada Act.
