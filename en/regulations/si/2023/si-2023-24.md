# Order Extending the Application of Section 14 of the Citizenship Act

Whereas, by the Order Extending the Application of Section 14 of that Act for a Period of Five Years<sup>a</sup>, the Minister of Citizenship and Immigration, under subsection 28.1(1)<sup>b</sup> of the Citizenship Act<sup>c</sup>, extended the application of section 14<sup>d</sup> of that Act for a period of five years, ending on July 31, 2024;

<sup>a</sup> SOR/2019-82

<sup>b</sup> S.C. 2014, c. 22, s. 27

<sup>c</sup> R.S., c. C-29

<sup>d</sup> S.C. 2017, c. 14, s. 8

And whereas, under subsection 28.1(2)<sup>b</sup> of that Act, the Minister may, before the expiry of each extended period, extend the application of section 14<sup>d</sup> for up to five years;

Therefore, the Minister of Citizenship and Immigration, under subsection 28.1(2)<sup>b</sup> of the Citizenship Act<sup>c</sup>, extends the application of section 14<sup>d</sup> of that Act until July 31, 2027.

Ottawa, June 27, 2023
