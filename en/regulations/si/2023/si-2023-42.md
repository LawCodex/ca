# Order Designating the Minister of Infrastructure and Communities as the Minister for the Purposes of the Canada Mortgage and Housing Corporation Act

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, under the definition *Minister* in section 2 of the Canada Mortgage and Housing Corporation Act<sup>a</sup>,

<sup>a</sup> R.S., c. C-7

- **(a)** repeals Order in Council P.C. 2021-947 of October 26, 2021<sup>b</sup>; and

  <sup>b</sup> SI/2021-92

- **(b)** designates the Minister of Infrastructure and Communities, a member of the King’s Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of that Act.
