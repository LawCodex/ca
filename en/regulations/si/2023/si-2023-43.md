# Order Designating the Minister of Infrastructure and Communities as the Minister for the Purposes of the National Housing Strategy Act

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, under section 3 of the National Housing Strategy Act<sup>a</sup>,

<sup>a</sup> S.C. 2019, c. 29, s. 313

- **(a)** repeals Order in Council P.C. 2021-948 of October 26, 2021<sup>b</sup>; and

  <sup>b</sup> SI/2021-93

- **(b)** designates the Minister of Infrastructure and Communities, a member of the King’s Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of that Act.
