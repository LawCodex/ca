# Order Dissolving the Canadian Securities Regulation Regime Transition Office

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, under subsection 17(1)<sup>a</sup> of the Canadian Securities Regulation Regime Transition Office Act<sup>b</sup>, dissolves the Canadian Securities Regulation Regime Transition Office effective March 30, 2023.

<sup>a</sup> S.C. 2013, c. 33, s. 133

<sup>b</sup> S.C. 2009, c. 2, s. 297
