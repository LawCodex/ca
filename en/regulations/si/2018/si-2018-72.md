# Transfer of Duties Order

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)<sup>a</sup> of the Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act<sup>b</sup>, transfers from the Minister of Canadian Heritage to the Minister of La Francophonie the powers, duties and functions of the Minister of Canadian Heritage under the Official Languages Act<sup>c</sup>.

<sup>a</sup> S.C. 2003, c. 22, s. 207

<sup>b</sup> R.S., c. P-34

<sup>c</sup> R.S., c. 31 (4th Supp.)
