# Order Transferring from the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, to the Minister of State to assist the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, the powers, duties and functions of the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development under certain Acts

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)<sup>a</sup> of the Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act<sup>b</sup>, transfers from the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, to the Minister of State to assist the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, the powers, duties and functions of the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development under the following Acts:

<sup>a</sup> S.C. 2003, c. 22, s. 207

<sup>b</sup> R.S., c. P-34

- **(a)** the Arctic Waters Pollution Prevention Act<sup>c</sup>;

  <sup>c</sup> R.S., c. A-12

- **(b)** the Canada Lands Surveys Act<sup>d</sup>;

  <sup>d</sup> R.S., c. L-6

- **(c)** the Canada Oil and Gas Operations Act<sup>e</sup>;

  <sup>e</sup> R.S., c. O-7; S.C. 1992, c. 35, s. 2

- **(d)** the Territorial Lands Act<sup>f</sup>;

  <sup>f</sup> R.S., c. T-7

- **(e)** the Dominion Water Power Act<sup>g</sup>;

  <sup>g</sup> R.S., c. W-4

- **(f)** the Canada Petroleum Resources Act<sup>h</sup>;

  <sup>h</sup> R.S., c. 36 (2nd Supp.)

- **(g)** the Gwich’in Land Claim Settlement Act<sup>i</sup>;

  <sup>i</sup> S.C. 1992, c. 53

- **(h)** the Nunavut Act<sup>j</sup>;

  <sup>j</sup> S.C. 1993, c. 28

- **(i)** the Nunavut Land Claims Agreement Act<sup>k</sup>;

  <sup>k</sup> S.C. 1993, c. 29

- **(j)** the Sahtu Dene and Metis Land Claim Settlement Act<sup>l</sup>;

  <sup>l</sup> S.C. 1994, c. 27

- **(k)** the Yukon First Nations Land Claims Settlement Act<sup>m</sup>;

  <sup>m</sup> S.C. 1994, c. 34

- **(l)** the Yukon Surface Rights Board Act<sup>n</sup>;

  <sup>n</sup> S.C. 1994, c. 43

- **(m)** the Mackenzie Valley Resource Management Act<sup>o</sup>;

  <sup>o</sup> S.C. 1998, c. 25

- **(n)** the Yukon Act<sup>p</sup>;

  <sup>p</sup> S.C. 2002, c. 7

- **(o)** the Nunavut Waters and Nunavut Surface Rights Tribunal Act<sup>q</sup>;

  <sup>q</sup> S.C. 2002, c. 10

- **(p)** the Tlicho Land Claims and Self-Government Act<sup>r</sup>;

  <sup>r</sup> S.C. 2005, c. 1

- **(q)** the Nunavut Planning and Project Assessment Act<sup>s</sup>;

  <sup>s</sup> S.C. 2013, c. 14, s. 2

- **(r)** the Northwest Territories Act<sup>t</sup>.

  <sup>t</sup> S.C. 2014, c. 2, s. 2
