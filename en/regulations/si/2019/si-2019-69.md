# Order Designating the Prime Minister as the Minister responsible for that Act

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to section 3 of the Intelligence Commissioner Act<sup>a</sup>, designates the Prime Minister as the Minister responsible for that Act.

<sup>a</sup> S.C. 2019, c. 13, s. 50
