# Order Designating the Minister of Citizenship and Immigration, who is a federal minister, to be the Minister referred to in that Act

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to section 3 of the College of Immigration and Citizenship Consultants Act<sup>a</sup>, designates the Minister of Citizenship and Immigration, who is a federal minister, to be the Minister referred to in that Act.

<sup>a</sup> S.C. 2019, c. 29, s. 292
