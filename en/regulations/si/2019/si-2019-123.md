# Transfer of Duties Order

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 2(a)<sup>a</sup> of the Public Service Rearrangement and Transfer of Duties Act<sup>b</sup>, transfers from the Minister of Science to the Minister of Industry the powers, duties and functions of the Minister of Science under Part I of the Budget Implementation Act, 1997<sup>c</sup> that were transferred to that Minister by Order in Council P.C. 2018-1013 of July 18, 2018<sup>d</sup>.

<sup>a</sup> S.C. 2003, c. 22, s. 207

<sup>b</sup> R.S., c. P-34

<sup>c</sup> S.C. 1997, c. 26

<sup>d</sup> SI/2018-69
