# Order Designating the Minister of Employment and Social Development to be the Minister for the Purposes of the Canadian Sustainable Jobs Act

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, under section 4 of the Canadian Sustainable Jobs Act<sup>a</sup>, effective May 1, 2026,

- **(a)** repeals Order in Council P.C. 2024-851 of June 27, 2024<sup>b</sup>; and

- **(b)** designates the Minister of Employment and Social Development, a member of the King’s Privy Council for Canada, to be the Minister for the purposes of the Canadian Sustainable Jobs Act<sup>a</sup>.

<sup>a</sup> S.C. 2024, c. 13

<sup>b</sup> SI/2024-34
