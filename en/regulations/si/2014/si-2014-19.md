# Canada Gazette Publication Order, 2014

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Justice, pursuant to subsection 10(2)<sup>a</sup> of the Statutory Instruments Act<sup>b</sup>, makes the annexed Canada Gazette Publication Order, 2014.

<sup>a</sup> S.C. 2000, c. 5, s. 58

<sup>b</sup> R.S., c. S-22

## Form and Manner of Publishing

### Section 1

The Canada Gazette is to continue to be published electronically in Portable Document Format (PDF).
