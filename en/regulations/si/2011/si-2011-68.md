# Order Designating the President of Shared Services Canada as Deputy Head in Respect of that Entity

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to paragraph 11(2)(a)<sup>a</sup> of the Financial Administration Act<sup>b</sup>, hereby designates the President of Shared Services Canada as deputy head in respect of that entity, effective August 4, 2011.

<sup>a</sup> S.C. 2003, c. 22, s. 8

<sup>b</sup> R.S., c. F-11
