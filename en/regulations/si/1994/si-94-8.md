# Order Designating the Minister for International Trade as Minister for Purposes of Sections 1 to 9 and Parts I and III of the Act

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to section 11 of the North American Free Trade Agreement Implementation Act<sup>\*</sup>, is pleased hereby to designate the Minister for International Trade, a member of the Queen’s Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of sections 1 to 9 and Parts I and III of that Act, effective January 1, 1994.

<sup>*</sup> S.C. 1993, c. 44
