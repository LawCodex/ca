# Order Authorizing the Minister of Transport to Prescribe Fees (Ship/Shore Communications Services)

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Transport and the Treasury Board, pursuant to paragraph 19(1)(b)<sup>\*</sup> of the Financial Administration Act, is pleased hereby to authorize the Minister of Transport to prescribe, by order, the charges to be paid by users of ship/shore communications services provided by Coast Guard Radio Stations.

<sup>*</sup> S.C. 1991, c. 24, s. 6
