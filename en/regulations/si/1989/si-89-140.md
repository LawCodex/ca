# Statutes of Canada Distribution Direction, No. 2

Direction Respecting the Distribution of the Acts of the Parliament of Canada

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Supply and Services, pursuant to subsection 10(2) of the Publication of Statutes Act, is pleased hereby to make the annexed Direction respecting the distribution of the Acts of the Parliament of Canada.

## Short Title

### Section 1

This Direction may be cited as the Statutes of Canada Distribution Direction, No. 2.

## Distribution

### Section 2

Four copies of the Acts of the Parliament of Canada shall be delivered or sent without charge to the chairperson or to one of the joint chairpersons of the Committee of the House of Commons, of the Senate or of both Houses of Parliament established for the purpose of reviewing and scrutinizing statutory instruments.
