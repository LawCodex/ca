# Order Designating Charlottetown, Prince Edward Island as Head Office of the Veterans Review and Appeal Board

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Veterans Affairs, pursuant to section 12 of the Veterans Review and Appeal Board Act<sup>a</sup>, hereby repeals Orders in Council P.C. 1985-1422 of April 25, 1985<sup>b</sup> and P.C. 1987-1568 of July 30, 1987<sup>c</sup> and designates Charlottetown, Prince Edward Island, as the head office of the Veterans Review and Appeal Board.

<sup>a</sup> S.C. 1995, c. 18

<sup>b</sup> SI/85-81

<sup>c</sup> SI/87-155
