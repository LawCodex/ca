# Secretary of State Authority to Prescribe Fees Order (Student Loans)

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Secretary of State of Canada and the Treasury Board, pursuant to paragraph 19.1(b)<sup>\*</sup> of the Financial Administration Act, is pleased hereby to authorize the Secretary of State of Canada to prescribe, by order, a fee based on a percentage of the amount of the loan, to be paid by full-time students to whom guaranteed student loans are made after July 31, 1991 under the Canada Student Loans Act.

<sup>*</sup> S.C. 1991, c. 24, s. 6
