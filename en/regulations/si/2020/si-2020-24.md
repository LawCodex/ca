# Order Designating the Minister of Labour as the Minister for the Purposes of that Act

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to section 5 of the Pay Equity Act<sup>a</sup>, designates the Minister of Labour, a member of the Queen’s Privy Council for Canada, as the Minister for the purposes of that Act.

<sup>a</sup> S.C. 2018, c. 27, s. 416
