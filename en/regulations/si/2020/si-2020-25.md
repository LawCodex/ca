# Order Designating the Minister of Industry, who is a Federal Minister, to be the Minister for the Purposes of that Act

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to section 4 of the College of Patent Agents and Trade-mark Agents Act<sup>a</sup>, designates the Minister of Industry, who is a federal minister, to be the Minister for the purposes of that Act.

<sup>a</sup> S.C. 2018, c. 27, s. 247
