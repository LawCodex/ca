# Order Designating the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development to be the Minister referred to in the Act

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to section 3 of the Canadian High Arctic Research Station Act<sup>a</sup>, designates the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development to be the Minister referred to in that Act.

<sup>a</sup> S.C. 2014, c. 39, s. 145
