# Order Designating the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness to be the Minister referred to in the Act

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to section 3 of the Prevention of Terrorist Travel Act<sup>a</sup>, designates the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness, who is a minister of the Crown, to be the Minister referred to in that Act.

<sup>a</sup> S.C. 2015, c. 36, s. 42
