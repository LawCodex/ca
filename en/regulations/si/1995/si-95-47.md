# Order Designating the Commission of Inquiry into the Deployment of Canadian Forces to Somalia as a Department and the Prime Minister as Appropriate Minister

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, is pleased hereby

- **(a)** pursuant to paragraph (b)<sup>\*</sup> of the definition ***department*** in section 2 of the Financial Administration Act, to designate the Commission of Inquiry into the Deployment of Canadian Forces to Somalia as a department for the purposes of that Act; and

  <sup>*</sup> S.C. 1992, c. 1, s. 69

- **(b)** pursuant to paragraph (b)<sup>\*</sup> of the definition ***appropriate Minister*** in section 2 of the Financial Administration Act, to designate the Prime Minister as the appropriate Minister with respect to the Commission of Inquiry into the deployment of Canadian Forces to Somalia.
