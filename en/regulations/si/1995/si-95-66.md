# Minister of Foreign Affairs Authority to Prescribe Fees Order

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Foreign Affairs and the Treasury Board, pursuant to paragraphs 19(1)(b)<sup>\*</sup> and 19.1(b)<sup>\*</sup> of the Financial Administration Act, is pleased hereby to repeal Order in Council P.C. 1989-1700 of August 24, 1989<sup>\*\*</sup> and to authorize the Minister of Foreign Affairs to prescribe, by order, the fee to be paid by any person to whom a permit or a certificate is issued pursuant to the Export and Import Permits Act.

<sup>*</sup> S.C. 1991, c. 24, s. 6

<sup>**</sup> SI/89-191, 1989 Canada Gazette Part II, p. 4013
