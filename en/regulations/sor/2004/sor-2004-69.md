# Canada Customs and Revenue Agency Regulations, No. 2

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to paragraph 36(1)(b) of the Public Service Employment Act, hereby makes the annexed Canada Customs and Revenue Agency Regulations, No. 2.

## Block Transfer

### Section 1

Subsections 37.3(1) and (2) of the Public Service Employment Act apply to the portion of the Public Service known as the Canada Customs and Revenue Agency.

## Cessation of Effect

### Section 2

These Regulations cease to have effect at 00:00:03, April 1, 2004.

## Coming into Force

### Section 3

These Regulations come into force at 00:00:01, April 1, 2004.
