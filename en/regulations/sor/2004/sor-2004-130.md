# Transportation Appeal Tribunal of Canada Certificate Regulations

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Transport, pursuant to section 7.92<sup>a</sup>, paragraph 8(b)<sup>b</sup> and subsection 8.1(4)<sup>c</sup> of the Aeronautics Act, hereby makes the annexed Transportation Appeal Tribunal of Canada Certificate Regulations.

<sup>a</sup> S.C. 2001, c. 29, s. 39

<sup>b</sup> S.C. 1992, c. 4, s. 21

<sup>c</sup> S.C. 2001, c. 29, s. 41

## Interpretation

### Section 1

In these Regulations, ***Act*** means the Aeronautics Act.

## Certificates

### Section 2

**(1)** The certificate referred to in section 7.92 of the Act shall be in the form set out in Schedule 1.

**(2)** The certificate referred to in paragraph 8(b) of the Act shall be in the form set out in Schedule 2.

**(3)** The certificate referred to in subsection 8.1(4) of the Act shall be in the form set out in Schedule 3.

## Repeal

### Section 3

[Repeal]

## Coming into Force

### Section 4

These Regulations come into force on the day on which they are registered.
