# Least Developed Country Tariff Withdrawal Order (2013 GPT Review)

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 38<sup>a</sup> of the Customs Tariff<sup>b</sup>, makes the annexed Least Developed Country Tariff Withdrawal Order (2013 GPT Review).

<sup>a</sup> S.C. 2011, c. 24, s. 119

<sup>b</sup> S.C. 1997, c. 36

## Withdrawal of Entitlement

### Section 1

Entitlement to the benefit of the Least Developed Country Tariff is withdrawn in respect of all goods that originate in Equatorial Guinea and Maldives.

## Exemption

### Section 2

Section 1 does not apply to goods that were in transit to Canada before January 1, 2015.
