# Federal Book Rebate (GST/HST) Regulations

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to paragraph (f)<sup>a</sup> of the definition ***specified person*** in subsection 259.1(1) and section 277<sup>b</sup> of the Excise Tax Act, hereby makes the annexed Federal Book Rebate (GST/HST) Regulations.

<sup>a</sup> S.C. 1997, c. 10, s. 69.1(1)

<sup>b</sup> S.C. 1993, c. 27, s. 125(1)

## Specified Persons

### Section 1

Any charity or qualifying non-profit organization listed in the schedule is prescribed for the purposes of paragraph (f) of the definition ***specified person*** in subsection 259.1(1) of the Excise Tax Act.

## Coming into Force

### Section 2

These Regulations are deemed to have come into force on October 24, 1996.
