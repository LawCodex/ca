# Most-Favoured-Nation Tariff Extension of Benefit Order — Ferro-chromium

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 23 of the Customs Tariff<sup>\*</sup>, is pleased hereby to extend the benefit of the Most-Favoured-Nation Tariff to ferro-chromium of tariff item No. 7202.41.00 or 7202.49.00 that originates in a country to which the General Tariff applies, effective April 15, 1990.

<sup>*</sup> R.S., c. 41 (3rd Supp.)
