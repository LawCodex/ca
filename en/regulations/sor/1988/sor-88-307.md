# New Brunswick Potato Marketing Levies (Interprovincial and Export) Order — No. 2

Order Respecting Fixing, Imposing and Collecting Levies on Potatoes Produced in New Brunswick and Marketed in Interprovincial and Export Trade

The New Brunswick Potato Agency, pursuant to sections 3<sup>\*</sup> and 4 of the New Brunswick Potato Order, made by Order in Council P.C. 1980-2344 of September 5, 1980<sup>\*\*</sup>, hereby makes the annexed Order respecting fixing, imposing and collecting levies on potatoes produced within a designated area in New Brunswick and marketed in interprovincial and export trade.

<sup>*</sup> SOR/85-144, 1985 Canada Gazette Part II, p. 1144

<sup>**</sup> SOR/80-726, 1980 Canada Gazette Part II, p. 3171

Florenceville, New Brunswick, May 31, 1988

## Short Title

### Section 1

This Order may be cited as the New Brunswick Potato Marketing Levies (Interprovincial and Export) Order — No. 2.

## Interpretation

### Section 2

In this Order,

***Commodity Board*** means the New Brunswick Potato Agency; (***Office***)

***designated area***[Repealed, SOR/2020-127, s. 1]

***person***[Repealed, SOR/90-692, s. 1]

***producer*** means a person who produces potatoes over at least four acres in any year. (***producteur***)

*SOR/90-692, s. 1; SOR/2019-273, s. 1; SOR/2020-127, s. 1*

## Application

### Section 3

This Order applies to the marketing of potatoes in interprovincial and export trade and to persons and property situated in the Province of New Brunswick.

## Imposition of Levy

### Section 4

Every producer shall pay to the Commodity Board a levy of $16.50 for each acre used in the production of potatoes by the producer or on their behalf.

*SOR/2019-273, s. 2; SOR/2020-127, s. 2*

## Method of Payment

### Section 5

A producer shall remit the levy referred to in section 4 to the Commodity Board within 30 days after the producer has sold his entire crop of potatoes.
