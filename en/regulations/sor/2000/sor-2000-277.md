# Deposit Insurance Application Fee By-law

The Board of Directors of the Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation, pursuant to paragraph 11(2)(g)<sup>a</sup> and subsection 18(1)<sup>b</sup> of the Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation Act, hereby makes the annexed Deposit Insurance Application Fee By-law.

June 29, 2000

<sup>a</sup> R.S. c. 18 (3rd Supp.), s. 51

<sup>b</sup> S.C. 1999, c. 28, s. 100

## Prescribed Fee

### Section 1

The prescribed fee that is to accompany an application for deposit insurance made by a provincial institution under subsection 17(1.1) of the Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation Act is $20,000 and is non-refundable.

*SOR/2006-235, s. 1*

## Coming into Force

### Section 2

This By-law comes into force on the day on which it is registered.
