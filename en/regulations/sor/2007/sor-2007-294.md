# Olympic and Paralympic Marks Regulations

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Industry, pursuant to subsection 12(1) of the Olympic and Paralympic Marks Act<sup>a</sup>, hereby makes the annexed Olympic and Paralympic Marks Regulations.

<sup>a</sup> S.C. 2007, c. 25

## Prescribed Period

### Section 1

For the purposes of paragraph 3(4)(a), subsection 4(1), paragraph 5(2)(a), section 6 and paragraph 8(2)(a) of the Olympic and Paralympic Marks Act, the prescribed period begins on the day on which these Regulations come into force and ends on December 31st, 2010.

## Coming into Force

### Section

These Regulations come into force on the day on which subsection 12(1) of the Olympic and Paralympic Marks Act comes into force.
