# Income Support Payment Amount Regulations

The Minister of Employment and Social Development, with the consent of the Minister of Finance, makes the annexed Income Support Payment Amount Regulations pursuant to subsection 7(2) of the Canada Emergency Response Benefit Act<sup>a</sup>.

<sup>a</sup> S.C. 2020, c. 5, s. 8

## Income Support Payment

### Section 1 — Amount of income support payment

For the purpose of subsection 7(1) of the Canada Emergency Response Benefit Act, the amount of an income support payment for any week is $500.

## Coming into Force

### Section 2 — Registration

These Regulations come into force on the day on which they are registered.
