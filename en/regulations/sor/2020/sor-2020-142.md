# Income Support Payment (Number of Weeks) Regulations

The Minister of Employment and Social Development, with the consent of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 8(2) of the Canada Emergency Response Benefit Act<sup>a</sup>, makes the annexed Income Support Payment (Number of Weeks) Regulations.

<sup>a</sup> S.C. 2020, c. 5, s. 8

Gatineau, June 26, 2020

Carla Qualtrough\
Minister of Employment and Social Development

## Maximum Number of Weeks

### Section 1 — 28 weeks

For the purpose of subsection 8(1) of the Canada Emergency Response Benefit Act, the maximum number of weeks for which income support payments may be made to a worker is 28 weeks.

*SOR/2020-189, s. 1*

## Coming into Force

### Section 2 — Registration

These Regulations come into force on the day on which they are registered.
