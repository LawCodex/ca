# Bait Services Fee Order

Order Prescribing the Fee to Be Paid for Bait Services Provided by the Department of Fisheries and Oceans

The Minister of Fisheries and Oceans, pursuant to Order in Council P.C. 1988-440 of March 10, 1988<sup>\*</sup>, hereby makes the annexed Order prescribing the fee to be paid for bait services provided by the Department of Fisheries and Oceans.

<sup>*</sup> SI/88-41, 1988 Canada Gazette Part II, p. 2010

Ottawa, March 29, 1996

FRED MIFFLIN

*Minister of Fisheries and Oceans*

## Short Title

### Section 1

This Order may be cited as the Bait Services Fee Order.

## Fee

### Section 2

The fee to be paid by a person to whom bait services are provided by the Department of Fisheries and Oceans is $ 0.60 per kilogram of bait.
