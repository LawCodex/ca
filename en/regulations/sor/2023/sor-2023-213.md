# Order Exempting Certain Navigable Waters Located in Quebec from the Application of Section 23 of the Canadian Navigable Waters Act

Whereas the Minister of Transport has received an application to exempt the navigable waters listed in the schedule to the annexed Order from the application of section 23<sup>a</sup> of the Canadian Navigable Waters Act<sup>b</sup>;

<sup>a</sup> S.C. 2019, c. 28, s. 57

<sup>b</sup> R.S., c. N-22; S.C. 2012, c. 31, s. 316; S.C. 2019, c. 28, s. 46

And whereas the Governor in Council is satisfied that this exemption would be in the public interest;

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Transport, makes the annexed Order Exempting Certain Navigable Waters Located in Quebec from the Application of Section 23 of the Canadian Navigable Waters Act under subsection 24(1)<sup>a</sup> of the Canadian Navigable Waters Act<sup>b</sup>.

## Exemption

### Section 1 — Navigable waters

The navigable waters listed in the schedule to this Order are exempt from the application of section 23 of the Canadian Navigable Waters Act.

## Coming into Force

### Section — Publication

This Order comes into force on the day on which it is published in the Canada Gazette, Part II.

<sup>*</sup> [Note: Order in force October 25, 2023.]
