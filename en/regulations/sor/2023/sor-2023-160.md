# Certain Russian Goods Remission Order

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, makes the annexed Certain Russian Goods Remission Order under section 115<sup>a</sup> of the Customs Tariff<sup>b</sup>.

<sup>a</sup> S.C. 2005, c. 38, par. 145(2)(j)

<sup>b</sup> S.C. 1997, c. 36

## Remission and Condition

### Section 1 — Remission

Remission is granted of the customs duties paid or payable under the Customs Tariff in respect of the following goods that originate in Russia:

- **(a)** goods that are classified under tariff item No. 2844.43.00 and that are imported during the period beginning on October 8, 2022 and ending on July 7, 2028; and

- **(b)** goods that are classified under tariff item No. 8807.20.00 and that are imported during the period beginning on March 2, 2022 and ending on April 16, 2029.

*SOR/2026-140, s. 1*

### Section 2 — Condition

**(1)** Remission is granted on condition that a claim for remission is made to the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness within two years after the day on which the goods are imported.

*Exception*\
**(2)** Despite subsection (1), in the case of goods referred to in paragraph (1)(b) that were imported before the day on which this subsection comes into force, remission is granted on condition that a claim for remission is made to the Minister of Public Safety and Emergency Preparedness within two years after that day.

*SOR/2026-140, s. 2*
