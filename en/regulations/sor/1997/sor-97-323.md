# CCFTA Rules of Origin for Casual Goods Regulations

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 13(2)<sup>a</sup> of the Customs Tariff<sup>b</sup>, hereby makes the annexed CCFTA Rules of Origin for Casual Goods Regulations.

<sup>a</sup> S.C. 1994, c. 47, s. 75(1)

<sup>b</sup> R.S., c. 41 (3rd Supp.)

## Interpretation

### Section 1

In these Regulations, ***casual goods*** means goods other than goods imported for sale or for an industrial, occupational, commercial or institutional or other like use.

## Casual Goods

### Section 2

Casual goods that are acquired in Chile are deemed to originate in Chile and are entitled to the benefit of the Chile Tariff if

- **(a)** the marking of the goods is in accordance with the marking laws of Chile and indicates that the goods are the product of Chile or Canada; or

- **(b)** the goods do not bear a mark and there is no evidence to indicate that the goods are not the product of Chile or Canada.

## Coming into Force

### Section

These Regulations come into force on the day on which section 9 of the Canada-Chile Free Trade Agreement Implementation Act, chapter 14 of the Statutes of Canada, 1997, comes into force.
