# Order Designating the Province of Quebec for the Purposes of the Definition “applicable guidelines” in Subsection 2(1) of the Divorce Act

Whereas the Province of Quebec, pursuant to subsection 2(5)<sup>a</sup> of the Divorce Act<sup>b</sup>, has established comprehensive guidelines for the determination of child support that deal with the matters referred to in section 26.1<sup>c</sup> of the Divorce Act<sup>b</sup>;

<sup>a</sup> S.C. 1997, c. 1, s. 1(4)

<sup>b</sup> R.S., c. 3 (2nd Supp.)

<sup>c</sup> S.C. 1997, c. 1, s. 11

Therefore, His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Justice, pursuant to subsection 2(5)<sup>a</sup> of the Divorce Act<sup>b</sup>, hereby makes the annexed Order Designating the Province of Quebec for the Purposes of the Definition “applicable guidelines” in Subsection 2(1) of the Divorce Act.

## Designation

### Section 1

The Province of Quebec is hereby designated for the purposes of the definition ***applicable guidelines*** in subsection 2(1) of the Divorce Act.

## Guidelines

### Section 2

For the purposes of subsection 2(5) of the Divorce Act, the following legislative texts are the laws that constitute the comprehensive guidelines for the Province of Quebec:

- **(a)** An Act to amend the Civil Code of Québec and the Code of Civil Procedure as regards the determination of child support payments, S.Q. 1996, c. 68;

- **(b)** the Regulation respecting the determination of child support payments, made by Order 484-97 of April 9, 1997;

- **(c)** Title Three of Book Two of the Civil Code of Quebec, S.Q. 1991, c. 64; and

- **(d)** Chapter VI.1 of Title IV of Book V of the Code of Civil Procedure, R.S.Q., c. C-25.

## Coming into Force

### Section 3

This Order comes into force on May 1, 1997.
