# General Export Permit No. 12 — United States Origin Goods

The Minister of Foreign Affairs, pursuant to subsections 7(1.1)<sup>a</sup> and 10(1)<sup>b</sup> of the Export and Import Permits Act, hereby issues the annexed General Export Permit No. 12 — United States Origin Goods.

<sup>a</sup> S.C. 1994, c. 47, s. 107

<sup>b</sup> S.C. 1994, c. 47, s. 111

Ottawa, January 24, 1997

LLOYD AXWORTHY

*Minister of Foreign Affairs*

## General

### Section 1

Subject to section 2, any person may, under the authority of this Permit, export from Canada any goods of United States origin as described in item 5400 of Group 5 of the Schedule to the Export Control List.

### Section 2

This Permit does not authorize the exportation of goods described in section 1 to any country listed in the Area Control List or to any of the following countries:

- **(a)** Cuba;

- **(b)** Democratic People’s Republic of Korea;

- **(c)** Iran; and

- **(d)** Syria.

*SOR/99-203, s. 1; SOR/2005-223, s. 1*

### Section 3

Where any goods exported under the authority of this Permit are required to be reported in the prescribed form under the Customs Act, the statement “GEP-12” or “LGE-12” shall be inserted in the appropriate field of the report.

## Repeal

### Section 4

General Export Permit No. Ex. 12 — United States Origin Goods<sup>1</sup> is cancelled.

<sup>1</sup> SI/89-120

## Coming into Force

### Section 5

This Permit comes into force on January 29, 1997.
