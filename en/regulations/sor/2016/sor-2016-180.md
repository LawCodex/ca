# Infant Feeding Bottle Nipples Regulations

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Health, pursuant to section 37 of the Canada Consumer Product Safety Act<sup>a</sup>, makes the annexed Infant Feeding Bottle Nipples Regulations.

<sup>a</sup> S.C. 2010, c. 21

## Interpretation

### Section 1 — Definition of ***infant feeding bottle nipple***

In these Regulations, ***infant feeding bottle nipple*** includes a similar product that is put in the mouth of an infant during use.

## Requirement

### Section 2 — Limit of volatile N-nitrosamines

An infant feeding bottle nipple must not contain more than 10 µg/kg total volatile N-nitrosamines, as determined by dichloromethane extraction.
