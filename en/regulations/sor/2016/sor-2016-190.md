# Residential Detectors Regulations

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Health, pursuant to section 37 of the Canada Consumer Product Safety Act<sup>a</sup>, makes the annexed Residential Detectors Regulations.

<sup>a</sup> S.C. 2010, c. 21

## Requirements

### Section 1 — Residential detectors

A residential detector set out in column 1 of the table to this section must comply with the standard set out in column 2, without regard to any other standard to which it refers.

**TABLE**

**Standards for Residential Detectors**

| Item | Column 1<br>Detector | Column 2<br>Standard |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Smoke detector | CAN/ULC-529, Smoke Detectors for Fire Alarm Systems, as amended from time to time |
| 2 | Heat actuated fire detector | CAN/ULC-530, Standard for Heat Detectors for Fire Protective Signaling Systems, as amended from time to time |
| 3 | Smoke alarm | CAN/ULC-531, Standard for Smoke Alarms, as amended from time to time |

*SOR/2026-113, s. 27*

### Section 2 — Components

A component of a residential fire alarm system set out in column 1 of the table to this section must comply with the standard set out in column 2, without regard to any other standard to which it refers.

**TABLE**

**Standards for Residential Fire Alarm System Components**

| Item | Column 1<br>Component | Column 2<br>Standard |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Audible signal device | CAN/ULC-525, Audible Signaling Devices for Fire Alarm and Signaling Systems, Including Accessories, as amended from time to time |
| 2 | Control unit | CAN/ULC-527, Standard for Control Units and Accessories for Fire Alarm Systems, as amended from time to time |

*SOR/2026-113, s. 28*

### Section 3 — Official languages

The information required under these Regulations to be provided on the label of a residential detector or in instructions intended for consumers must appear in English and in French.
