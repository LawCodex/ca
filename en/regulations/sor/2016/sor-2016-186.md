# Ice Hockey Helmet Regulations

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Health, pursuant to section 37 of the Canada Consumer Product Safety Act<sup>a</sup>, makes the annexed Ice Hockey Helmet Regulations.

<sup>a</sup> S.C. 2010, c. 21

## Requirement

### Section 1 — Standard

Ice hockey helmets must meet the requirements of standard Z262.1, entitled Ice hockey helmets, published by the Canadian Standards Association, as amended from time to time.
