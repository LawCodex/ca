# Central Registry of Divorce Proceedings Fee Order

Order Prescribing the Fee To Be Paid by any Person to whom a Service is Provided under the Central Registry of Divorce Proceedings Regulations

The Minister of Justice, pursuant to Order in Council P.C. 1986-851 of April 10, 1986<sup>\*</sup> made pursuant to subsection 27(1) of the Divorce Act, 1985<sup>\*\*</sup>, hereby makes the annexed Order prescribing the fee to be paid by any person to whom a service is provided under the Central Registry of Divorce Proceedings Regulations, effective June 1, 1986.

<sup>*</sup> SI/86-55, 1986 Canada Gazette Part II, p. 1925

<sup>**</sup> S.C. 1986, c. 4

Ottawa, May 22, 1986

JOHN CROSBIE\
*Minister of Justice*

## Short Title

### Section 1

This Order may be cited as the Central Registry of Divorce Proceedings Fee Order.

## Fee

### Section 2

**(1)** Subject to subsection (2), the fee to be paid by a person who files an application for divorce and in respect of whom a service is provided under section 5 of the Central Registry of Divorce Proceedings Regulations is $10 per application.

**(2)** No fee shall be paid by a person who receives legal aid from a province in respect of the person’s application for divorce where, pursuant to the law of the province, payment by that person of the fees established by the province for filing an application for divorce is waived.

*SOR/86-614, s. 1; SOR/86-835, s. 1*
