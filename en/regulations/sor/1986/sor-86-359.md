# Delegation of Powers (Customs) Regulations

Regulations Delegating Powers Conferred by Section 163 of the Customs Act Upon the Minister of National Revenue

The Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to subsection 163(2) of the Customs Act, hereby revokes the Delegation of Powers (Customs) Regulations made on the 12th of October, 1984<sup>\*</sup> and, in substitution therefor, makes the annexed Regulations delegating powers conferred by section 163 of the Customs Act upon the Minister of National Revenue.

<sup>*</sup> SOR/84-807, 1984 Canada Gazette Part II, p. 3965

Ottawa, March 25, 1986

ELMER MacKAY\
*Minister of National Revenue*

## Short Title

### Section 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Delegation of Powers (Customs) Regulations.

## General

### Section 2

The officers named in the schedule may exercise the powers conferred upon the Minister of National Revenue by section 163 of the Customs Act.
