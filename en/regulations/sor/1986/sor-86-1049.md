# Energy Administration Act Sections 53 to 65 Non-application Order, 1986

Order Declaring Sections 53 to 65 of the Energy Administration Act To Be Non-Applicable in Respect of any Producer-Province

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Energy, Mines and Resources, pursuant to subsection 52(1.1)<sup>\*</sup> of the Energy Administration Act<sup>\*\*</sup>, is pleased hereby to make the annexed Order declaring sections 53 to 65 of the Energy Administration Act to be non-applicable in respect of any producer-province, effective November 1, 1986.

<sup>*</sup> S.C. 1986, c. 39, s. 5

<sup>**</sup> S.C. 1980-81-82-83, c. 114, s. 2

## Short Title

### Section 1

This Order may be cited as the Energy Administration Act Sections 53 to 65 Non-application Order, 1986.

## Declaration

### Section 2

Sections 53 to 65 of the Energy Administration Act do not apply in respect of any producer-province.
