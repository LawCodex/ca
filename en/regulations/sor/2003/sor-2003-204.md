# Continuation of Amalgamated or Merged Corporations Regulations

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to paragraph 304(1)(o) of the Excise Act, 2001<sup>a</sup>, hereby makes the annexed Continuation of Amalgamated or Merged Corporations Regulations.

<sup>a</sup> S.C. 2002, c. 22

## Prescribed Purposes

### Section 1

For the purposes of section 213 of the Excise Act, 2001, the purpose of applying any provision of Parts 5 and 6, other than section 213, of that Act is a prescribed purpose.

## Coming into Force

### Section 2

These Regulations take effect on July 1, 2003.
