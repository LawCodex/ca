# Sandy Bay Band Council Elections Order

Whereas the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development deems it advisable for the good government of the Sandy Bay Band that the council of the Band, consisting of a chief and councillors, shall, beginning on February 26, 2003, be selected by elections to be held in accordance with the Indian Act;

Therefore, the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, pursuant to subsection 74(1) of the Indian Act, hereby makes the annexed Sandy Bay Band Council Elections Order.

Gatineau, Quebec, February 25, 2003

Robert D. Nault

Minister of Indian Affairs

and Northern Development

## Elections

### Section 1

The council of the Sandy Bay Band, consisting of a chief and councillors, shall, beginning on February 26, 2003, be selected by elections to be held in accordance with the Indian Act.

## Coming into Force

### Section 2

This Order comes into force on the day on which it is registered.
