# Toronto Port Authority Regulations

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Transport, pursuant to paragraph 62(1)(b) of the Canada Marine Act<sup>a</sup>, hereby makes the annexed Toronto Port Authority Regulations.

<sup>a</sup> S.C. 1998, c. 10

## Prohibition

### Section 1

The Toronto Port Authority shall not use, or authorize or permit another person to use, the port to build a bridge or similar fixed link between the mainland of the City of Toronto and the Toronto Islands.

## Exception

### Section 2

Section 1 does not apply in respect of a pedestrian tunnel.

*SOR/2011-200, s. 1*
