# Order Specifying that the Canadian Forces are a Portion of the Public Sector Employing One Hundred or more Employees for the Purpose of Subsection 4(1) of that Act

Whereas, pursuant to paragraph 4(1)(d) of the Employment Equity Act<sup>a</sup>, the Treasury Board has consulted the Minister of Labour and the Minister of National Defence concerning the application of that Act to the Canadian Forces;

<sup>a</sup> S.C. 1995, c. 44

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Treasury Board, and the Minister of Labour, pursuant to paragraph 4(1)(d) of the Employment Equity Act<sup>a</sup>, hereby specifies that the Canadian Forces are a portion of the public sector employing one hundred or more employees for the purpose of subsection 4(1) of that Act.
