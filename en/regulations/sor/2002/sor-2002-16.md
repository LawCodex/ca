# Radiocommunication Act (Paragraph 9(1)(c)) Exemption Order (National Defence and Security)

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Industry, pursuant to subsection 3(2)<sup>a</sup> of the Radiocommunication Act<sup>b</sup>, hereby makes the annexed Radiocommunication Act (Paragraph 9(1)(c)) Exemption Order (National Defence and Security).

<sup>a</sup> S.C. 1989, c. 17, s. 4

<sup>b</sup> S.C. 1989, c. 17, s. 2

## Exemption

### Section 1

Subject to section 2, Her Majesty in right of Canada is exempt from the application of paragraph 9(1)(c) of the Radiocommunication Act.

## Conditions

### Section 2

The exemption under section 1 applies only if the encrypted subscription programming signal or encrypted network feed

- **(a)** is decoded for the purposes of national defence or security; and

- **(b)** is not available from a lawful distributor.

## Coming into Force

### Section 3

This Order comes into force on the day on which it is registered.
