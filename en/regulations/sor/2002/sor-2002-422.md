# Order Specifying the Royal Canadian Mounted Police for the Purposes of the Employment Equity Act

Whereas, pursuant to paragraph 4(1)(d) of the Employment Equity Act<sup>a</sup>, the Treasury Board has consulted the Solicitor General of Canada concerning the application of that Act to the Royal Canadian Mounted Police;

<sup>a</sup> S.C. 1995, c. 44

Therefore, Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Labour and the Treasury Board, pursuant to paragraph 4(1)(d) of the Employment Equity Act<sup>a</sup>, hereby makes the annexed Order Specifying the Royal Canadian Mounted Police for the Purposes of the Employment Equity Act.

## Designation

### Section 1

The Royal Canadian Mounted Police is specified under paragraph 4(1)(d) of the Employment Equity Act as a portion of the public sector employing one hundred or more employees to which that Act applies.

## Coming into Force

### Section 2

This Order comes into force on the day on which it is registered.
