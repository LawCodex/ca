# Order Fixing the Day for the Purposes of Section 144 of the Economic Action Plan 2014 Act, No. 1

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Labour, pursuant to section 144 of the Economic Action Plan 2014 Act, No. 1<sup>a</sup>, makes the annexed Order Fixing the Day for the Purposes of Section 144 of the Economic Action Plan 2014 Act, No. 1.

<sup>a</sup> S.C. 2014, c. 20

## General

### Section 1 — Day for purposes of section 144

The day for the purposes of section 144 of the Economic Action Plan 2014 Act, No. 1<sup>1</sup> is fixed as June 1, 2019.

<sup>1</sup> S.C. 2014, c. 20

## Coming into Force

### Section — Coming into force

This Order comes into force on the first day on which sections 111, 139 and 140 of the Economic Action Plan 2014 Act, No. 1<sup>1</sup> are all in force, but if it is registered after that day, it comes into force on the day on which it is registered.

<sup>*</sup> [Note: Order in force February 11, 2015, *see* SI/2015-5 and SI/2015-6.]

<sup>1</sup> S.C. 2014, c. 20
