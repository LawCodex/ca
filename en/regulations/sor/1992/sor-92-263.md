# Security Certificate Transfer Fee (Cooperative Credit Associations) Regulations

Regulations Prescribing the Maximum Fee that an Assocition May Charge for a Security Certificate Issued in Respect of a Transfer

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 92(2) and section 463 of the Cooperative Credit Associations Act<sup>\*</sup>, is pleased hereby to make the annexed Regulations prescribing the maximum fee that an association may charge for a security certificate issued in respect of a transfer, effective June 1, 1992.

<sup>*</sup> S.C. 1991, c. 48

## Short Title

### Section 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Security Certificate Transfer Fee (Cooperative Credit Associations) Regulations.

## Maximum Fee

### Section 2

An association may charge a fee not exceeding five dollars for a security certificate issued in respect of a transfer.
