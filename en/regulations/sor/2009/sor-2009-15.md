# Regulations Adding Perfluorooctane Sulfonate and Its Salts to the Virtual Elimination List

The Minister of the Environment and the Minister of Health, pursuant to subsection 3(1) of the Perfluorooctane Sulfonate Virtual Elimination Act<sup>a</sup>, hereby make the annexed Regulations Adding Perfluorooctane Sulfonate and Its Salts to the Virtual Elimination List.

<sup>a</sup> S.C. 2008, c. 13

Ottawa, December 7, 2008

JIM PRENTICE\
*Minister of the Environment*

Ottawa, January 9, 2009

LEONA AGLUKKAQ\
*Minister of Health*

## Addition

### Section 1

Perfluorooctane sulfonate and its salts are added to column 1 of the Virtual Elimination List<sup>1</sup>, compiled under subsection 65(2) of the Canadian Environmental Protection Act, 1999<sup>2</sup>.

<sup>1</sup> SOR/2006-298

<sup>2</sup> S.C. 1999, c. 33

## Coming into Force

### Section 2

These Regulations come into force on the day on which they are registered.
