# Equivalent Courses (GST/HST) Regulations

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 277(1)<sup>\*</sup> of the Excise Tax Act, is pleased hereby to make the annexed Regulations prescribing equivalents of courses that follow a curriculum designated by a school authority.

<sup>*</sup> S.C. 1990, c. 45, s. 12

## Section 1

[Repealed, SOR/2002-277, s. 5]

## Prescribed Courses

### Section 2

For the purpose of section 9 of Part III of Schedule V to the Excise Tax Act, music lessons are prescribed equivalents of music courses that follow a curriculum designated by a school authority.

*SOR/94-368, s. 4*
