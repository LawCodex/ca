# Reportable Diseases Regulations

Regulations Prescribing Certain Diseases as Reportable Diseases

The Minister of Agriculture, pursuant to subsection 2(2) of the Health of Animals Act<sup>\*</sup>, hereby makes the annexed Regulations prescribing certain diseases as reportable diseases.

<sup>*</sup> S.C. 1990, c. 21

Dated at Ottawa, the 10th day of December, 1990

DON MAZANKOWSKI\
*Minister of Agriculture*

## Short Title

### Section 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Reportable Diseases Regulations.

## Reportable Diseases

### Section 2

The diseases set out in the schedule are prescribed as reportable diseases for the purpose of section 2 of the Health of Animals Act.
