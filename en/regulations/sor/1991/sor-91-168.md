# Canadian Heraldic Authority Fee Order

Order Prescribing the Fee for Processing Applications Submitted to the Canadian Heraldic Authority for the Grant of Coats of Arms

The Prime Minister, pursuant to Order in Council P.C. 1990-2224 of October 11, 1990<sup>\*</sup>, hereby makes the annexed Order prescribing the fee for processing applications submitted to the Canadian Heraldic Authority for the grant of coats of arms.

<sup>*</sup> SI/90-153, 1990 Canada Gazette Part II, p. 4613

Ottawa, February 27, 1991

BRIAN MULRONEY\
*Prime Minister*

## Short Title

### Section 1

This Order may be cited as the Canadian Heraldic Authority Fee Order.

## Fee

### Section 2

The fee payable by an applicant for the processing of an application submitted to the Canadian Heraldic Authority for the grant of a coat of arms is $435.

### Section 3

The fee referred to in section 2 shall be paid by cheque payable to the Receiver General for Canada and shall be included with a letter acknowledging receipt of notice from the Canadian Heraldic Authority that a warrant has issued authorizing the grant of a coat of arms to the applicant.
