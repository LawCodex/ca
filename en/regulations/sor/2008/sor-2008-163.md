# Material Banking Group Percentage Regulations

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 978<sup>a</sup> of the Bank Act<sup>b</sup>, hereby makes the annexed Material Banking Group Percentage Regulations.

<sup>a</sup> S.C. 2005, c. 54, s. 135

<sup>b</sup> S.C. 1991, c. 46

## Prescribed Percentage

### Section 1 — Prescribed percentage

For the purposes of subsection 508(2) of the Bank Act, the percentage is 35%.

### Section 2 — Prescribed percentage

For the purposes of subsection 508(3) of the Bank Act, the percentage is 50%.
