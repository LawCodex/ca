# Designated Public Office Holder Regulations

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the President of the Treasury Board, pursuant to subsection 2(1)<sup>a</sup> and paragraph 12(c.1)<sup>b</sup> of the Lobbying Act<sup>c</sup>, hereby makes the annexed Designated Public Office Holder Regulations.

<sup>a</sup> S.C. 2006, c. 9, s. 67

<sup>b</sup> S.C. 2006, c. 9, s. 79

<sup>c</sup> R.S., c. 44 (4th Supp); S.C. 2006, c. 9, s. 66

## Section 1

For the purposes of paragraph (c) of the definition ***designated public office holder*** in subsection 2(1) of the Lobbying Act, the positions and classes of positions set out in the schedule are designated as positions occupied by a designated public office holder.

## Section 2

These Regulations come into force on July 2, 2008.

## SCHEDULE (Section 1)
