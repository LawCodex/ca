# Transfer of Portions of the Canadian Food Inspection Agency Regulations

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to subsection 123(1) of the Public Service Employment Act<sup>a</sup>, hereby makes the annexed Transfer of Portions of the Canadian Food Inspection Agency Regulations.

<sup>a</sup> S.C. 2003, c. 22, ss. 12 and 13

## Block Transfer

### Section 1

Subsection 132(1) of the Public Service Employment Act applies to all persons employed or engaged in the portions of the federal public administration in the Canadian Food Inspection Agency known as the Email, Data Centre and Network Services Unit and the Email, Data Centre and Network Services Support Unit.

## Coming into Force

### Section 2

These Regulations come into force on November 15, 2011.
