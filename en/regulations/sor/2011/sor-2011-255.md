# Communications Security Establishment Regulations

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Prime Minister, pursuant to subsection 123(1) of the Public Service Employment Act<sup>a</sup>, hereby makes the annexed Communications Security Establishment Regulations.

<sup>a</sup> S.C. 2003, c. 22, ss. 12 and 13

## Block Transfer

### Section 1

Subsection 132(1) of the Public Service Employment Act applies to the Communications Security Establishment.

## Cession of Effect

### Section 2

These Regulations cease to have effect at 00:00:03 on November 16, 2011.

## Coming into Force

### Section 3

These Regulations come into force at 00:00:01 on November 16, 2011.
