# Maximum Amount for Agreements with Governments Order

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development, pursuant to paragraph 73(2.1)(b)<sup>a</sup> of the Nunavut Act<sup>b</sup>, hereby makes the annexed Maximum Amount for Agreements with Governments Order.

<sup>a</sup> S.C. 1998, c. 15, s. 13(3)

<sup>b</sup> S.C. 1993, c. 28

## Maximum Amount

### Section 1

For the purposes of paragraph 73(2.1)(b) of the Nunavut Act, an amount of $15,000,000 is hereby prescribed for agreements with governments.

## Coming into Force

### Section 2

This Order comes into force on the day on which it is registered.
