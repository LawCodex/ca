# Regulations Designating Certain Countries for the Purposes of Tariff Item No. 9810.00.00, 1999-1

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to paragraph 132(1)(g) of the Customs Tariff<sup>a</sup>, hereby makes the annexed Regulations Designating Certain Countries for the Purposes of Tariff Item No. 9810.00.00, 1999-1.

<sup>a</sup> S.C. 1997, c. 36
