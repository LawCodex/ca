# Exemption from Deposit Insurance By-law (Exemption Fee)

The Board of Directors of the Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation, pursuant to paragraphs 11(2)(g)<sup>a</sup> and 26.03(1)(e)<sup>b</sup> of the Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation Act, hereby makes the annexed Exemption from Deposit Insurance By-law (Exemption Fee).

<sup>a</sup> R.S., c. 18 (3rd Supp.), s. 51

<sup>b</sup> S.C. 1997, c. 15, s. 114

September 29, 1999

The Minister of Finance, pursuant to section 26.05<sup>b</sup> of the Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation Act, hereby approves the annexed Exemption from Deposit Insurance By-law (Exemption Fee) made by the Board of Directors of the Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation.

Ottawa, October 4, 1999

## Fee Payable

### Section 1

The fee payable under paragraph 26.03(1)(e) of the Canada Deposit Insurance Corporation Act by a federal member institution that applies under section 26.02 of that Act for authorization to accept deposits without being a member institution is $20,000.

*SOR/2008-105, s. 1*

## Coming into Force

### Section 2

This By-law comes into force on October 15, 1999.
