# Lawrence Bay Airways Ltd. Exemption Order, 1989

Order Respecting the Exemption of Lawrence Bay Airways Ltd. from the Application of Subparagraph 72(2)(a)(i) of the National Transportation Act, 1987

Whereas, pursuant to section 73 of the National Transportation Act, 1987<sup>\*</sup>, the Minister of Transport considers it necessary or advisable in the public interest that a domestic licence be issued to Lawrence Bay Airways Ltd. who, under subparagraph 72(2)(a)(i) of that Act, is not qualified to obtain the licence.

<sup>*</sup> R.S., c. 28 (3rd Supp.)

Therefore, the Minister of Transport, pursuant to section 73 of the National Transportation Act, 1987<sup>\*</sup>, hereby revokes the Lawrence Bay Airways Ltd. Exemption Order, 1988, made on December 31, 1988<sup>\*\*</sup>, and makes the annexed Order respecting the exemption of Lawrence Bay Airways Ltd. from the application of subparagraph 72(2)(a)(i) of the National Transportation Act, 1987, in substitution therefor.

<sup>**</sup> SOR/89-79, 1989 Canada Gazette Part II, p. 883

Ottawa, August 9, 1989

BENOÎT BOUCHARD\
*Minister of Transport*

## Short Title

### Section 1

This Order may be cited as the Lawrence Bay Airways Ltd. Exemption Order, 1989.

## Exemption

### Section 2

Lawrence Bay Airways Ltd. is hereby exempted from the application of subparagraph 72(2)(a)(i) of the National Transportation Act, 1987.
