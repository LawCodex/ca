# Expiry of Section 12.2 of the Softwood Lumber Products Export Charge Act, 2006 Regulations

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of National Revenue, pursuant to paragraph 101(a) and section 102 of the Softwood Lumber Products Export Charge Act, 2006<sup>a</sup>, makes the annexed Expiry of Section 12.2 of the Softwood Lumber Products Export Charge Act, 2006 Regulations.

<sup>a</sup> S.C. 2006, c. 13

## Section 1 — Ceasing to be in force

Section 12.2 of the Softwood Lumber Products Export Charge Act, 2006 ceases to be in force on October 12, 2013.

## Section 2 — Coming into force

These Regulations are deemed to have come into force on October 13, 2013.
