# Exemption for Public Notices or Documents (Cooperative Credit Associations) Regulations

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 463(1)<sup>a</sup> and section 487.14<sup>b</sup> of the Cooperative Credit Associations Act<sup>c</sup>, hereby makes the annexed Exemption for Public Notices or Documents Regulations (Cooperative Credit Associations) Regulations.

<sup>a</sup> S.C. 2005, c. 54, s. 208

<sup>b</sup> S C. 2005, c. 54, s. 212

<sup>c</sup> S.C. 1991, c. 48

## Interpretation

### Section 1

In these Regulations, ***Act*** means the Cooperative Credit Associations Act.

## Exemption Circumstances

### Section 2

For the purpose of section 487.14 of the Act, the prescribed circumstances are that the exemption does not prejudice any of the members or the shareholders or the public interest.

## Coming into Force

### Section 3

These Regulations come into force on June 1, 2011.
