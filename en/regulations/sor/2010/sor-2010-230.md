# Annual Statement (Banks and Bank Holding Companies) Regulations

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsections 308(2)<sup>a</sup>, 840(2)<sup>b</sup> and 978(1)<sup>c</sup> of the Bank Act<sup>d</sup>, hereby makes the annexed Annual Statement (Banks and Bank Holding Companies) Regulations.

<sup>a</sup> S.C. 2005, c. 54, s. 69

<sup>b</sup> S.C. 2005, c. 54, s. 126

<sup>c</sup> S.C. 2005, c. 54, s. 135

<sup>d</sup> S.C. 1991, c. 46

## Contents of Annual Statement

### Section 1

The prescribed statements referred to in subsections 308(2) and 840(2) of the Bank Act are

- **(a)** a balance sheet as at the end of the financial year;

- **(b)** a statement of income for the financial year;

- **(c)** a statement of cash flows for the financial year;

- **(d)** a statement of changes in shareholders’ equity for the financial year; and

- **(e)** a statement of comprehensive income for the financial year.

*SOR/2014-273, s. 14(F); SOR/2015-29, s. 1*

## Coming into Force

### Section 2

These Regulations come into force on June 1, 2011.
