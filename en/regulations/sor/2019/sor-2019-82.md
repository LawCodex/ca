# Order Extending the Application of Section 14 of that Act for a Period of Five Years

The Minister of Citizenship and Immigration, pursuant to subsection 28.1(1)<sup>a</sup> of the Citizenship Act<sup>b</sup>, extends the application of section 14 of that Act for a period of five years, ending on July 31, 2024.
