# Order Calling in Certain Notes

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to paragraph 9(1)(b)<sup>a</sup> of the Currency Act<sup>b</sup>, makes the annexed Order Calling in Certain Notes.

<sup>a</sup> S.C. 2018, c. 12, s. 230

<sup>b</sup> R.S., c. C-52

## Section 1 — Notes called in

Notes issued under the Bank of Canada Act in the following denominations are called in:

- **(a)** one dollar;

- **(b)** two dollars;

- **(c)** twenty-five dollars;

- **(d)** five hundred dollars; and

- **(e)** one thousand dollars.

## Section 2 — Coming into force

This Order comes into force on January 1, 2021.
