# MicroSD Cards Exclusion Regulations (Copyright Act)

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Industry, pursuant to sections 79<sup>a</sup> and 87<sup>b</sup> of the Copyright Act<sup>c</sup>, makes the annexed MicroSD Cards Exclusion Regulations (Copyright Act).

<sup>a</sup> S.C. 2001, c. 27, s. 240

<sup>b</sup> S.C. 1997, c. 24, s. 50

<sup>c</sup> R.S., c. C-42

## Microsd Cards

### Section 1

Memory cards in microSD form factor, including microSD, microSDHC and microSDXC cards, are excluded from the definition ***audio recording medium*** in section 79 of the Copyright Act.

## Coming into Force

### Section 2

These Regulations come into force on the day on which they are registered.
