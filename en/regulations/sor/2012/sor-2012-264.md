# Regulations for the Redemption of One Cent Coins

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 9.01(1)<sup>a</sup> of the Currency Act<sup>b</sup>, makes the annexed Regulations for the Redemption of One Cent Coins.

<sup>a</sup> S.C. 2012, c. 19, s. 389

<sup>b</sup> R.S., c. C-52

## Section 1 — Authorization

The Minister is authorized to redeem one cent coins that are or that have at any time been current in Canada.

## Section 2 — Coming into force

These Regulations come into force on the day of their registration.
