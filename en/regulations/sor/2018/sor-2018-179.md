## Drug Screening Equipment

### Section 1 — Approved equipment

For the purpose of the definition *approved drug screening equipment* in section 320.11 of the Criminal Code, the following equipment that is designed to ascertain the presence of a drug in a person’s body is approved:

- **(a)** a Dräger DrugTest® 5000 and a Dräger DrugTest® 5000 STK-CA, when used together; and

- **(b)** a SoToxa™, an Abbott SoToxa™ Test Cartridge and an Abbott SoToxa™ Oral Fluid Collection Device, when used together.

*SOR/2019-237, s. 1*

## Coming into Force

### Section 2 — Day made

This Order comes into force on the day on which it is made.
