# Quebec Hog Marketing Levies (Interprovincial and Export Trade) Order

Les Éleveurs de porcs du Québec, formerly known as the Fédération des producteurs de porc du Québec, pursuant to sections 3 and 4 of the Quebec Hog Marketing Order<sup>a</sup>, makes the annexed Quebec Hog Marketing Levies (Interprovincial and Export Trade) Order.

Longueuil, February 19, 2018

<sup>a</sup> SOR/79-727

## Interpretation

### Section 1

The following definitions apply in this Order:

***producer*** means a person engaged in the production of hogs, sows or boars in Quebec. (***producteur***)

***sow or boar*** means a sow or boar produced in Quebec. (***truie ou verrat***)

## Levies

### Section 2

Every producer shall pay the following levies to the Commodity Board for each hog, sow or boar sold or marketed by the producer in interprovincial or export trade:

- **(a)** per hog whose net weight is at least 65 kg, a levy of $0.0123 per kilogram of *net weight of the hot carcass* as defined in the Règlement sur la production et la mise en marché des porcs, RLRQ, c. M-35.1, r. 281; and

- **(b)** per sow or boar, a levy of $8.97.

## Remittance of Levies

### Section 3

The producer shall remit the levies referred to in section 2 to the Commodity Board on or before the 15th day of each month in respect of hogs, sows and boars sold or marketed during the preceding month.

## Repeal

### Section 4

The Quebec Hog Marketing Levies (Interprovincial and Export Trade) Order<sup>1</sup> is repealed.
