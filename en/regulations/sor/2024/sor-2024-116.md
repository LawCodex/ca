# Order Grouping Ministers’ Offices for the Purpose of a Pay Equity Plan

Her Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Labour, makes the annexed Order Grouping Ministers’ Offices for the Purpose of a Pay Equity Plan under subsection 173(1) of the Pay Equity Act<sup>a</sup>.

<sup>a</sup> S.C. 2018, c. 27, s. 416

## Section 1 — Grouping of ministers’ offices

All ministers’ offices are grouped for the purpose of establishing and updating a single pay equity plan.

## Section 2 — Coming into force

This Order comes into force on the day on which it is registered.
