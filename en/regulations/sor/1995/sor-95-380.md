# Safeguard Surtax Regulations, 1995-2

Regulations Prescribing Agricultural Goods in Respect of Any Country for the Purposes of Imposing a Safeguard Surtax, 1995-2

His Excellency the Governor General in Council, on the recommendation of the Minister of Finance, pursuant to subsection 60.01(7)<sup>\*</sup> of the Customs Tariff<sup>\*\*</sup>, is pleased hereby to make the annexed Regulations prescribing agricultural goods in respect of any country for the purpose of imposing a safeguard surtax, 1995-2, effective August 1, 1995.

<sup>*</sup> S.C. 1994, c. 47, s. 83

<sup>**</sup> R.S., c. 41 (3rd Supp.)

## Short Title

### Section 1

These Regulations may be cited as the Safeguard Surtax Regulations, 1995-2.

## Goods Subject to Safeguard Surtax

### Section 2

For the purposes of section 68 of the Customs Tariff, the following agricultural goods, classified under the tariff item numbers that follow them, are prescribed in respect of any country:

- **(a)** cream (0401.30.10, 0401.30.20);

- **(b)** powdered whey (0404.10.21, 0404.10.22); and

- **(c)** butter and other fats and oils derived from milk (0405.10.10, 0405.10.20, 0405.90.10, 0405.90.20).

*SOR/98-28, s. 24*
